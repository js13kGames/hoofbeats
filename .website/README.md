In an arena of darkness, the unicorn who shines the brightest reigns supreme! Tackle foes head on or snipe them from afar and capture every color of the rainbow for yourself! Remain in control and aim for a high score!

MOVE: WASD or Arrow Keys or Touch
DASH: Space, J, Z, or Shift
SHOOT: E, K, X, or Q
