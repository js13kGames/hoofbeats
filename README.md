# HOOFBEATS

js13kGames jam game! In an arena of darkness, the unicorn who shines the brightest reigns supreme! Tackle foes head on or snipe them from afar and capture every color of the rainbow for yourself! Remain in control and aim for a high score!

A silly little take on a sort of slither.io kind of game. Reeeeally wanted to get multiplayer in, but what can you do... hope people have fun with it anyway!

a game by aria unicornfan.com

## Controls

MOVE: WASD or Arrow Keys or Touch
DASH: Space, J, Z, or Shift
SHOOT: E, K, X, or Q
